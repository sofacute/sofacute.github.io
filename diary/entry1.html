an archive of my second life, in which i spend most of my time running the art community region 'conejo island'

i started taking screenshots regularly around 2018, and by 2019 when conejo was founded i was taking thousands of pictures

its a simple act of creative expression to me..something i can do easily without thought and keep going no matter the life circumstances. i love to capture the candid beauty of second life and its inhabitants as well as document all the beautiful simple things about virtual communities that may get taken for granted and lost to time.


my original diary spans 9 gigs, the pictures shared here are only from the moment i moved computers in 2023 forward. i may share those old photos sometime~but probably not through here

my avatar is called Waterfairy (often referred to as 'wafa') ..~shes a million year old interdimensional deity that created an interdimensional resort for magic fantasy whatever creatures of all kinds. i passively roleplay as her, she is me but not, the 'second self', although there are appearances from other avatars of mine here and there

the CASA CONEJO website needs a big overhaul & its information is out of date so if you are curious about CONEJO, its best to visit in-world. the region name is easy to find, just type 'CONEJO ISLAND' on the map or search, walk around and learn :)


disclaimer (because there has been a lot of confusion, and lack of clarity on my part), i am very very sensitive and fickle with social energy. i dont react well to feelings of forced friendship. CONEJO is for others to enjoy, i want people to hang out freely. its a big place and i dont see myself as the main attraction of CONEJO. i understand the desire to be a part of it, our community as a whole is very welcoming, but friendship with me requires time, natrualness and consent.. i ask to be careful and slow with your approach, and evaluate your intentions. remember that for the people who administer CONEJO its our second home, the council members have involved for years and the subjects i tend to photograph in my diary are people who ive built some degree trust with. that being said i can be a much more open person during events.. dont take this as 'don't come to CONEJO'..do come to CONEJO, respect us and we will respect you!
