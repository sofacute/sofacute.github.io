this is a semi unfiltered archive of all the happenings in my second life which mostly compromises of running the art community region 'conejo island'

i started taking screenshots regularly around 2018, and by 2019 around when conejo was founded i was taking thousands of pictures

its mostly just expression through photography for me, but i also like to capture the beauty of second life and its inhabitants as well as document all the beautiful simple things about virtual communities that may get taken for granted and lost to time


my original diary spans 9 gigs, the pictures shared here are only from the moment i moved computers in 2023 forward. i may share those old photos sometime~but probably not through here
