INSPIRATION
i've been asked about what my inspirations are quite a few times.. sometimes i dont like answering that, but i do have things to say that inspire me
it seems suited for its own page
this is just a narrow view, i'll try to be brief with each thing
i'll add one thing at a time
  • 1. ANIME CHARACTERS..
    getting into anime at around 8 years old was the biggest impact on my art..

    despite being raised catholic,
    the first time i felt i understood a spiritual feeling was when i came across Shugo Chara, then Hatsune Miku, then 'moe' as a whole. no exaggeration here

    the feeling that i got from anime girls was beyond anything and it helped me get through the world and break past barriers and cages i had developed in my mind

    a social shame almost fully destroyed the magic feelings, and alongside trying to shed the love for anime girls came a deep disdain for spirituality & a more pronounced bitterness/disgust for life.. i've healed that aspect long since and it feels pure again

    now i have my own world and my own characters that give me that feeling
    it doesn't heal everything.. but it really gets me through and inspires me

    i owe it to miku & company!
