ELABORATION ON THE WORK
a little bit of context on the stuff
  • RGB PAW UNIVERSE
    the universe i work with in my subconsious and my fiction!
    a little complicated, but it can be summed up with: an alien dimension led by three rabbit girl deities
  • RABBITMAIDEN SOFTWORKS
    my visual novel series that takes place in the RGB PAW UNIVERSE!
    each visual novel is a 'mirror' into a story

    i'm working in chronological order, they are loosely connected
    i do everything except the music..

  • HEARTLOVEPOWERTEMPLE
    the first rabbitmaiden softworks entry!
    6 chapters, roughly a 4 hour read.. about 4 priestesses in VENUS.. "LOVEMAIDENS"!
    my first vn. a light yuri. worked on it from 2021 and released in 2022. a prequel to 8eyes!
    this story i first thought to make as a webpage before making a vn instead
  • SPECIAL GIRLS STORY
    the second rabbitmaiden softworks entry..
    the story has technically been cooking since 2020 when i wrote the original idea for the story.

    when i made the decision to start making vns,
    i decided it was too ambitious for a first entry
    (maybe it still is too ambitious! but well it is coming)
    i've been working on it for 3 years.. it will be two volumes (mirrors, vns), 4 chapters each
    the first volume is novel-length,

    6 priestesses in JUPITER.. "FAIRYMAIDENS"!

    its almost done but i still have a lot left. im hoping to have it out within the next year. its the most complex & layered story i've done so far

  • 8EYES
    what started it all.. maybe my most popular work
    its about girlworld, which is an inbetween dimension on earth
    the main characters are 4 girls who all have red eyes and are out liers in girlworld because they don't have traditional powers


    late 2016 i came up with the idea
    it was initially some webcomics i did to fuck around, i didn't really intend for people to see it..
    i was still shedding a shame i had about drawing anime girls, and i was starting to freshly re-explore my tastes.
    looking back this is when i first started tapping into 'rgb paw universe', though i didn't know it at the time


    2017 i made the tumblr-blog to put it up and share it with friends.
    2018 it suddenly spread and went slightly viral. some of that old version still circulates. i ended up quitting that one, and then bringing it back as a 'webproject' in 2019.

    it moved away from being a comic and became more a "text&pictures" kind of thing, like a proto-vn

    it isn't supposed to be a story, it's more of a 'window into a world'
    the last update was 2021
    i don't know if i'll update it again. it is part of the universe, and has connection, so i might return to it someday in some way..

  • CONEJO & SECOND LIFE DIARY
    i play a lot of second life!
    its been a big part of my life & art since 2018..
    CONEJO ISLAND is the place i run, i founded it and maintain it with the help of some friends.
    i've been putting my sl photo diary up on here for a bit now
    it's just my life and its not a story or anything scripted, but its still my art.
  • PRECIOUS THEATRE
    a visual novel i made with lady cpu under the name heavensmiles!
    an experimental vn pilot made in 2022
    its called experimental because it was an experiment!

    we really needed money, so we whipped it up in about 3 months.
    i made all the character designs, and cpu adapted them into 3d

    we wrote and concieved everything together, also assigning certain characters to eachother for the dialogue with sort of roleplaying.
    i really love this world and the characters. we talk about going back to it sometime but we'll see. it may not happen.

  • HEAVENSMILES
    lady cpu & i's collaborations.. if you see the banner, it means we we're involved together.
    so far we've just done PRECIOUS THEATRE and VN CUP but we work together in other casual ways a lot
