the template i made for myself..

i roll for the words with kolourpaint open, and type the letters and everything into it.
kolourpaint is amazing, so its very enjoyable to do.

at first i played it on paper, but my handwriting is pretty bad.
playing on paper is fun tho because you can do it anywhere if you have the dice

anyway, to make up for the lack of paper's charm.. i added space to my template for putting pictures of anything. i just do stuff from files or stuff thats related to the words or real life..

~if theres extra space from rolling smaller numbers, i tend to add another image..
it may break out into low-effort collage mode. it all depends on the mood.

i often opt for an anime girl off vndb's front page roulette

this is an open source game, if you can call it that! just in case anybody feels like they wanna play this themselves..you may use this template, make your own, or tweak the rules according to different dice
