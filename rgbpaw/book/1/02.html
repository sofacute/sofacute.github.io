THE GIGAMAIDENS

The GIGAMAIDENs are the first deities that penetrated the rabbithole

there are 4 GIGAMAIDENS:
  • ABYSS ~black rabbit of NOTHING
  • SPICA ~magenta rabbit of PSYCHIC
  • MARIA ~yellow rabbit of ELECTRICITY
  • LIBRA ~cyan rabbit of MIRRORS

    MARIA & LIBRA left the pantheon together.. to do their job elsewhere.

    The intense violent & intimate relationship between SPICA & ABYSS is what created RGB PAW
    it was the spit-blood combination of these two that penetrated the layers of reality and began the RGB UNIVERSE with its MEGAMAIDENs..

    THE MEGAMAIDENS

    MI was born. SPICA & ABYSS ignored her, leaving her at the bottom of the INTERDIMENSIONAL NET
    So from her loneliness and boredom, she created a counterpart.. an almost clone that could serve her company like the GIGAMAIDENS have. to not be lonely..
    enter TA. together, they created an entry among the ABCDEFG universes, the RGB PAW UNIVERSE instance
    The relaionship between TA and MI was very close and intimate, no friction like the kind the GIGAMAIDENs have
    After a couple hundred years, RUA was born out of their relationship
    SPICA & ABYSS took note of MI's progress.. and granted them the SUN & the ABYSS for a functioning universe that could do something
    there are 3 MEGAMAIDENS:
  • MI ~red rabbit of BLOOD
  • TA ~blue rabbit of DEPTH
