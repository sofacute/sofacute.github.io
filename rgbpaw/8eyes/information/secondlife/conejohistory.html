CONEJO HISTORY
CONEJO ISLAND is now a region and a thriving art community, i will use this page to recall the history of it for documentation purposes.

here is the description of CONEJO as it is in second life:

'a haven & creative circle for animals angels demons magic-persons creatures nonhuman etc. '
relax freely around conejos landscape

OCTOBER 3rd 2019
i began to rent a 4096 sqm square of land on the maindland region of Lendrum

this land was floating above, so i used prefab pieces of land in a square shape. eventually i settled for a grassy corner with a sandy area.
this wasn't my first time owning land, but it was still very early for me in my experience with building

in the beginning this was simply my home. i called it 'sofa island', and did not have plans for a community.. still, that is what i wanted. i played alone a lot, did not find myself fitting into the existing communities of second life. i had some friends visit time to time but it was nothing like what conejo is like now.
